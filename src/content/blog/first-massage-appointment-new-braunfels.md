Your first appointment should feel straightforward. You will fill out a short intake, we will confirm goals and any health considerations, and then you will get a session paced for your comfort — not a surprise “upsell tour.”

If you are booking a house call, the main difference is setup: I bring the table and supplies, and we choose a quiet room with enough space to work safely.

When you are ready, book online and choose the session type that best matches what you want that day. If you are unsure, therapeutic massage is a strong default, and we can adjust on the table.
